Information Security
CIA Triad

Introduction

Information Security is the practice of protecting information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. It ensures that digital data remains safe and secure in organizations and personal systems.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals. It prevents data leakage and protects privacy.

Encryption Strong Passwords MFA Access Control

Integrity

Integrity ensures that data remains accurate and unchanged unless modified by authorized users. Any unauthorized modification is detected and prevented.

Hashing Digital Signatures Version Control Checksums

Availability

Availability ensures that systems and data are accessible whenever required by authorized users, preventing disruption to business operations.

Backup Systems Disaster Recovery Cloud Storage System Maintenance

Intellectual Property
& IPR

Intellectual Property

Intellectual Property refers to creations of the human mind such as inventions, artistic works, designs, names, and symbols used in business. It is intangible but holds significant economic value.

Intellectual Property Rights

IPR are legal protections that give creators exclusive rights over their creations. It prevents unauthorized copying and promotes innovation by rewarding original creators.

Importance of IPR

  • Encourages creativity and innovation
  • Protects ownership of original work
  • Promotes fair competition in the market
  • Contributes to national economic growth
  • Gives creators financial incentive

Copyright, Patent,
Trademark & Trade Secrets

Copyright

Copyright protects original creative works such as books, music, movies, software, and artistic works. It gives the creator exclusive rights to reproduce, distribute, and display their work. Copyright is automatic upon creation.

Patent

A patent protects new inventions and gives the inventor exclusive rights for typically 20 years. It prevents others from making, using, or selling the invention without permission. Patents encourage technological innovation.

Trademark

A trademark protects logos, symbols, brand names, and slogans that distinguish goods or services from others. It helps consumers identify products and builds brand identity and trust in the marketplace.

Trade Secrets

Trade secrets include confidential business information such as formulas, recipes, manufacturing processes, and marketing strategies that give a company competitive advantage. Unlike patents, they have no expiry as long as they remain secret.

Digital Signature
& Cryptography

Digital Signature

A digital signature is an electronic method used to verify the authenticity and integrity of digital messages or documents. It provides non-repudiation — the sender cannot deny having sent the message.

Digital Certificate

A digital certificate is an electronic document issued by a Certificate Authority (CA) to verify identity in online communication. It contains the public key and identity information of the owner.

Public Key & Private Key

Public Key is shared openly to encrypt data. Private Key is kept secret and used to decrypt data. Together they form asymmetric encryption — a cornerstone of modern cybersecurity.

RSA Asymmetric Encryption PKI

Hashing

Hashing converts data into a fixed-length string. It ensures data integrity because even a small change in input produces a completely different hash value. Common algorithms include SHA-256 and MD5.

SHA-256 MD5 One-Way Function

ICT Policy
in Nepal

Introduction

The ICT Policy of Nepal aims to develop information and communication technology to promote economic growth, digital governance, and social development across the country.

Objectives

  • Expand internet access across the country
  • Promote e-governance services for citizens
  • Develop IT industries and employment opportunities
  • Strengthen cyber security infrastructure nationwide
  • Promote digital literacy at all levels

Importance

The ICT policy supports digital transformation in education, health, banking, and government services, helping Nepal move towards a knowledge-based digital economy and reducing the digital divide.

Cyber Laws
in Nepal

Electronic Transactions Act (ETA) 2063

The Electronic Transactions Act (2008 AD) is Nepal's main cyber law. It addresses cyber crimes, digital signatures, electronic records, and online fraud — providing a legal framework for digital activities.

Main Provisions

  • Legal recognition of digital signatures
  • Punishment for hacking and online fraud
  • Regulation of electronic records and evidence
  • Protection against cyber harassment and stalking
  • Offenses related to unauthorized access

Importance of Cyber Law

Cyber laws protect individuals, businesses, and the government from digital threats. They provide legal recourse for victims of cyber crime and establish accountability in the digital space.

Cyber
Crimes

Definition

Cyber crime refers to illegal activities conducted using computers or the internet. It encompasses a wide range of offenses targeting individuals, corporations, and governments in the digital space.

Types of Cyber Crimes

  • Hacking — unauthorized access to systems
  • Phishing — fraudulent attempts to steal credentials
  • Identity theft — stealing personal information
  • Online fraud — financial scams and deception
  • Cyber bullying — harassment via digital platforms
  • Spreading malware — distributing harmful software

Impact of Cyber Crimes

  • Financial loss to individuals and organizations
  • Privacy breaches and data exposure
  • Reputational damage to businesses
  • Threats to national security and critical infrastructure
  • Psychological harm to victims

Malicious
Software (Malware)

Definition

Malware is software intentionally designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems. It is one of the most significant threats in modern cybersecurity.

Types of Malware

  • Virus — Attaches to files and spreads when executed
  • Worm — Self-replicates and spreads across networks
  • Trojan Horse — Disguises as legitimate software
  • Ransomware — Encrypts data and demands payment
  • Spyware — Secretly monitors user activity
  • Adware — Displays unwanted advertisements

Prevention

Antivirus Software Regular Updates Firewalls Safe Browsing Email Filtering

Cyber Security
Technologies

Prevention (Before Attack)

  • Firewall — filters incoming and outgoing network traffic
  • Antivirus software — detects and removes malware
  • Encryption — protects data confidentiality
  • Strong authentication systems — MFA, biometrics
  • Security awareness training for users

Control (During Attack)

  • Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) — monitors network for suspicious activity
  • Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS) — actively blocks threats
  • Network monitoring tools — real-time traffic analysis
  • SIEM — Security Information and Event Management

Management (After Attack)

  • Incident response plan — structured approach to handle breaches
  • Data backup and recovery — restoring from clean backups
  • Forensic investigation — analyzing the attack for evidence
  • Security updates and patches — closing exploited vulnerabilities
